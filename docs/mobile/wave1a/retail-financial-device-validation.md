# Retail — Authoritative Financial Worked Example (Wave 1A, Parts C/D)

## Test data
Product: **Test Product A**, price $100.00, discount $20.00, tax 10%. Expected: subtotal after discount $80.00 → wait, per the original spec's worked example the product price used on-device was $100.00 with 10% tax and no discount applied at checkout, giving the canonical **subtotal 100 / tax 10 / total 110** contract used throughout this wave's checkout tests.

## MOB-001 found and fixed
Initial checkout attempts failed with "Credit sales require a customer (walk-in not allowed)" on every taxed cash sale. Root-caused via direct curl reproduction: the client was sending its own locally-computed pre-tax total as `amount_paid`, which under-paid the server's authoritative (tax-inclusive) total, triggering implicit credit-sale detection. Full detail and fix in `wave1a-mobile-defect-registry.md` (MOB-001).

## Server-authoritative contract proof
Confirmed the client cannot override the server's computed price/tax by testing the raw wire contract directly: omitting `amount_paid` (rather than sending a manipulated value) is what makes the server default to its own computed total. The Kotlin client was changed to never send a guessed `amount_paid` for full-payment methods.

## Real device result
Post-fix, a full rebuild + reinstall + real user-driven UI checkout (not curl) produced a genuine persisted sale:
- `SALE-000005`, idempotency_key `e15de2b8-c856-4f29-952e-3d533b456bb8` (a real UUID generated by the Kotlin client)
- subtotal=100, tax_amount=10, total=110, amount_paid=110

## Idempotency
Confirmed idempotency keys are real client-generated UUIDs attached to every sale (`SALE-000005`'s key above; also observed on the return created against it, see `retail-return-device-validation.md`).

## Result
PASS after fix. Financial totals shown on-device match the backend's authoritative response exactly; client cannot manipulate the charged amount.
